# Calls to action

One CTA per video, matched to a single goal. The spoken CTA is part of the cut (keep it short, ≤ 3 s). The on-screen
card is `plan.cta.text`, shown for the last `cta.duration` (default 2.8 s) unless `cta.start` is set.

## Pick the goal
| Goal | When | EN | PT-BR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Follow (series) | Multi-part content, open loops | "Follow for part 2" | "Me segue pra parte 2" |
| Follow (value) | Evergreen tips | "Follow for one {topic} tip a day" | "Me segue pra uma dica de {topic} por dia" |
| Comment keyword | Lead magnet, DMs, engagement | "Comment GUIDE and I'll send it" | "Comenta GUIA que eu te mando" |
| Comment opinion | Contrarian or comparison hooks | "Which one do you use? 👇" | "Qual você usa? 👇" |
| Save | Lists, tutorials, references | "Save this for later" | "Salva pra não perder" |
| Share | Relatable or POV content | "Send this to someone who…" | "Manda pra alguém que…" |
| Link in bio | Product, app, newsletter | "Link in bio to try it" | "Link na bio pra testar" |
| Watch next | Series, longer YouTube video | "Full video on my channel" | "Vídeo completo no canal" |

## Rules
- Earn it first: put the CTA after the payoff, never before it. Exception: a mid-video "save this" right after a
  list starts.
- Loop-friendly endings: make the last line flow back into the first (the "infinite loop" trick). This raises
  rewatches and is often better than a hard CTA on very short videos (< 15 s).
- Avoid "like and subscribe" boilerplate; the CTA should give a reason ("…for part 2", "…so you don't lose it").
- Keep the card text ≤ 6 words. Emoji are OK in the card, but avoid them in the spoken line.
- Comment-keyword CTAs need automation (ManyChat or similar) or a real reply plan. Ask the user before using them.
- The CTA must sit inside the safe zone. The card is placed at the top (`Card` style, top margin 300 px), clear of
  the bottom UI.
